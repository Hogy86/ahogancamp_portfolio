# Assets

This directory contains **supporting assets** used across projects and documentation.

Assets exist to help **communicate ideas clearly**, not as primary deliverables themselves.

---

## What You’ll Find Here

Assets may include:
- Images or diagrams used in documentation
- PDFs such as slide decks or exported reports
- Visuals supporting case studies or explanations

They are typically referenced from:
- Project READMEs
- Documentation in the `docs/` directory
- Presentations or written summaries

---

## How These Assets Are Used

From a product perspective, these assets help:
- Make complex ideas easier to understand
- Support stakeholder communication
- Provide visual context alongside written explanations

They are intentionally kept separate from code and notebooks to maintain a clear separation between **content** and **implementation**.

---

## Notes

Assets are supporting materials and may not always be perfectly up to date on their own. The authoritative context for their use is always provided in the surrounding documentation or project README.
