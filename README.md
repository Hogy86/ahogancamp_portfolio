## How I Approach Product Work

My product philosophy is consistent across initiatives:

- **Start with the problem, not the solution**  
  Technology is a tool, not the goal.

- **Bias toward learning early**  
  Validate assumptions quickly with lightweight experiments and prototypes.

- **Make tradeoffs explicit**  
  Document why decisions are made, not just what was chosen.

- **Optimize for outcomes**  
  Success is measured by impact, adoption, and clarity—not feature count.

Not every artifact here represents a finished product. Many are **discovery efforts, proofs of concept, or decision‑support work**, which is where much of the most important product thinking happens.
