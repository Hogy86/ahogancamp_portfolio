# Prototype Notebooks

This directory contains notebooks used for **targeted prototyping** once a problem has been reasonably framed.

These notebooks are designed to answer **specific feasibility or value questions**, rather than explore broadly.

---

## Purpose

Prototype notebooks are typically used to answer questions like:

- Can this approach work with available data?
- What level of performance or accuracy is realistic?
- How might a user or system interact with this capability?
- What are the major constraints or risks?

They support decision‑making before investing in fuller implementation.

---

## Characteristics of These Notebooks

- Narrower scope than exploration notebooks
- Focused on a specific approach or hypothesis
- More structured than early exploration
- Designed to produce a clear signal, not perfection

Code quality may improve compared to exploration, but the emphasis remains on **learning quickly**, not polish.

---

## How These Notebooks Fit Into Product Work

Prototype notebooks often lead to:
- Product or technical go/no‑go decisions
- Refinement of requirements
- Identification of follow‑on engineering work
- Clear articulation of tradeoffs

Successful prototypes are typically translated into project work elsewhere in the repository.
