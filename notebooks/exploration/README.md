# Exploration Notebooks

This directory contains notebooks used for **early‑stage exploration**.

The goal of these notebooks is to **understand the problem space**, not to produce a finished solution.

---

## Purpose

Exploration notebooks are typically created to answer questions such as:

- What is actually happening in the data or system today?
- Are our assumptions valid?
- Where are the biggest unknowns or risks?
- What information do we need before committing to a direction?

They often precede formal requirements, designs, or roadmaps.

---

## Characteristics of These Notebooks

- Broad, open‑ended analysis
- Multiple hypotheses explored
- Dead ends documented (intentionally)
- Inline notes explaining reasoning and observations

This work helps avoid premature commitment and reduces downstream rework.

---

## How These Notebooks Are Used

Outputs from exploration notebooks often inform:
- Product discovery and framing
- Stakeholder conversations
- Go / no‑go decisions
- What to prototype next

They are intentionally lightweight and disposable once learning objectives are met.
