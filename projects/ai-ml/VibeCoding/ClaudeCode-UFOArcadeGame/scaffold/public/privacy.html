Shield vs Robots — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

What the Shield vs Robots Android App Stores

The Shield vs Robots Android app does not collect or share any data. It stores only the following information locally on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your best score: the highest score you have ever achieved.
  • Control setting: your preference for the touch-button layout (default or swapped).
  • Help overlay setting: whether you have seen the "How to Play" screen (shown once per install).
  • Gameplay counters: anonymous event counts, such as sessions started, levels reached, power-ups caught, runs restarted, game-over events, victories, and shield catches.

All of this information stays on your device only. It is never sent to any server, external service, or third party. The app does not connect to the internet. It requires no account, sign-in, or permissions.

This information stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Shield vs Robots → Storage → Clear storage). It is not included in device backups. On the website, clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

What the Shield vs Robots Website Stores

The web version (hosted on GitHub Pages) stores your best score and anonymous gameplay event counters in your browser's localStorage only. Settings (control layout, help overlay state) are not stored by the website. Nothing is sent to any server or third party.

What the App Does Not Do

  • Does not collect your name, email, location, or any personal identifier.
  • Does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Does not track you across apps or devices.

Website Hosting

Shield vs Robots is also available as a web game on GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages may log technical request data (such as your IP address) under GitHub's own privacy statement.
