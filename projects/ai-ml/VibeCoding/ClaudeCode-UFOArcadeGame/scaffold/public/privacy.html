Privacy policy

Shield vs Robots collects and shares no data. The app and website store three values only on your own device: your best score, your control settings, and anonymous gameplay counters kept only on this device. None of this is ever sent anywhere.

The app works fully offline and needs no account, sign-in, or permission.
