Shield vs Robots — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

What the Shield vs Robots Android App Collects

The Shield vs Robots Android app collects no data. The app stores only the following information locally on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your best score: the highest score you have ever achieved.
  • Control setting: your preference for the touch-button layout (default or swapped).
  • Help overlay setting: whether you have seen the "How to Play" screen (shown once per install).
  • Gameplay counters: anonymous counts of levels reached, power-ups caught, and runs started.

All of this information stays on your device only. It is never sent to any server, external service, or third party. The app does not connect to the internet. It requires no account, sign-in, or permissions.

What the App Does Not Do

  • Does not collect your name, email, location, or any personal identifier.
  • Does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Does not track you across apps or devices.

Website Hosting

Shield vs Robots is also available as a web game on GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages may log technical request data (such as your IP address) under GitHub's own privacy statement. The web version stores best score, settings, and counters in your browser's localStorage only and does not transmit this data anywhere.
