# Market Research Methods

Inherits invoking subagent's model.

## User segmentation
- Define 2-4 concrete segments (not "everyone") with distinguishing
  needs/behaviors.
- For each: primary pain point, current workaround/alternative, and
  what "better" looks like to them.

## Competitive analysis
- Identify direct competitors (same problem, similar solution) and
  indirect ones (same problem, different approach, including "do
  nothing").
- For each: what they do well, where they fall short, and the gap this
  product could fill.

## Success metrics
- Metrics must be measurable and time-bound (e.g., "reduce onboarding
  time from 15 to 5 minutes within 2 releases," not "improve
  onboarding").
- Separate business metrics (revenue, retention, adoption) from user
  metrics (task completion time, error rate, satisfaction).

## Use case prioritization
- Rank by: (a) how many segments it serves, (b) how acute the pain point
  is, (c) feasibility given likely technical constraints.
- Each use case gets a one-line justification tied to a segment and pain
  point — no orphan use cases.
