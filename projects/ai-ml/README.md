
# AI & Machine Learning Projects

This folder contains product‑oriented projects exploring **AI, machine learning, and LLM‑adjacent capabilities**.

The emphasis here is not on model novelty, but on **evaluating feasibility, value, and adoption** from a product perspective.

---

## What These Projects Demonstrate

- Identifying where AI meaningfully solves a user or business problem
- Rapid prototyping to validate assumptions early
- Evaluating tradeoffs such as:
  - Accuracy vs usability
  - Cost vs value
  - Automation vs human‑in‑the‑loop
- Translating technical capabilities into product decisions

---

## Typical Questions Addressed

- Is this problem actually suitable for ML or AI?
- What level of accuracy or confidence is “good enough”?
- How would users interact with or trust this system?
- What are the operational or cost implications?

---

## Artifacts You’ll See

- Lightweight prototypes and proofs of concept
- Exploratory analysis to test assumptions
- Documentation of decisions and tradeoffs
- Notes on adoption risks and constraints

These projects are intentionally exploratory and represent **early‑stage product discovery and validation**, not polished production systems.
