# Analytics & Reporting Projects

This folder contains projects focused on **analytics, reporting, and decision support**, with an emphasis on turning data into **actionable insight**.

The work here highlights how product decisions are informed by data—not driven blindly by it.

---

## What These Projects Demonstrate

- Translating business questions into analytical approaches
- Defining metrics that reflect real outcomes, not vanity numbers
- Balancing flexibility for users with consistency and governance
- Communicating insights clearly to non‑technical stakeholders

---

## Typical Questions Addressed

- What decisions are we trying to support?
- What data is actually trustworthy and available?
- How much precision is necessary to move forward?
- How should insights be surfaced for different audiences?

---

## Artifacts You’ll See

- Analytical notebooks and explorations
- Reporting structures or mock dashboards
- Documentation explaining assumptions and limitations
- Examples of iterative refinement based on feedback

These projects reflect **product‑driven analytics**, where the goal is better decisions—not just more charts.
